Common questions

Where is my team's data kept?
Entirely on your iPhone. Even Season has no accounts, no sign-up and no server — nothing you enter leaves your device. That's also why the App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”.
What happens if I delete the app?
Because everything lives on your phone, deleting the app deletes your squads, games and seasons with it. Share a season report as a PDF first if you want a permanent record.
I paid for the unlock and got a new phone — do I pay again?
No. The unlock is tied to your Apple ID. On the new phone, reach the unlock card and tap “Already paid? Restore purchase”. Your game data itself doesn't transfer automatically (it lives on the old device), so export any season reports you want to keep.
How does the free trial work?
Your first two complete games are free. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks every future game and the season report — no subscription, nothing recurring.
Why did the live clock stop before we finished?
The clock stops itself once a half reaches its set length, so a forgotten phone can't record a 191-minute game. Tap End half or Full time when the whistle goes. If your games genuinely run longer, raise the half length in team settings before kick-off.
Does keeper time count as playing?
Yes — a keeper played the half. Within a single game the rotation also gives keepers their fair run on the field, and the season ledger counts all of it.

Privacy
